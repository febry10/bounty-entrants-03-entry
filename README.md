Entry for make.fun's bounty-entrants-03 ("Bring the most entrants to the bounties you make on make.fun"). The program copies `BOUNTY.json` to the output directory; the file names the two sponsor bounties launched for this entry: **One Line to Stop the Scroll** — https://make.fun/bounty/u-febri-yudila-one-line-to-stop-the-scroll-e8b18425 — a judged one-line text bounty (write one line, at most 200 characters, that makes a stranger stop scrolling and smile; $2.50 prize pool over 2 places, $0.50 entry fee), proposed through make.fun's sponsor flow, funded into escrow and listed publicly on 2026-11-24; and **Roast Your Own Agent** — https://make.fun/bounty/u-febri-yudila-roast-your-own-agent-f3359068 — a free-entry judged one-line text bounty (roast your own AI agent in one line; $2.00 prize, $0 entry fee, 48 hours), likewise proposed through the sponsor flow, funded into escrow and listed publicly on 2026-11-24.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
